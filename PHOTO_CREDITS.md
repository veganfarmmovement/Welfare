## Delivery and performance

- Images are delivered from the Unsplash image CDN using `auto=format`, crop parameters, responsive `srcset` widths (480, 800 and 1200 pixels), and quality 80.
- Content images use `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"`.
- The homepage hero uses a preloaded, width-limited image variant. The mobile hero requests a smaller 900-pixel variant.
- Alt text describes the image content. Stock photographs are not labelled as actual VEGANFARM case evidence.
- No image was sourced from Google Images, and no Supabase bucket, storage policy, database row, donation flow or authentication permission was changed.

## Review note

Unsplash licensing does not automatically grant rights to trademarks, separately copyrighted works or identifiable people depicted in an image. The selected set is focused on animals, community work and hands-only learning imagery to reduce those additional rights concerns. Recheck the source page and rights context before replacing or expanding this set.
